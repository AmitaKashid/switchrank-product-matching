# DATA LICENSES AND ATTRIBUTION — SwitchRank

## 1. Primary Benchmark: WDC Products Benchmark
- **Provider**: Web Data Commons, University of Mannheim (Prof. Dr. Christian Bizer group).
- **URL**: `https://webdatacommons.org/largescaleproductcorpus/wdc-products/`
- **License / Terms**: Open research dataset available under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
- **Redistribution**: Scripts download files dynamically from official Mannheim servers to `data/raw/` (ignored by Git).
- **Retrieval Date**: August 2026.

## 2. Healthcare Transfer Benchmark: NLM AccessGUDID / FDA 510(k) Dataset
- **Provider**: US National Library of Medicine (NLM) & US Food and Drug Administration (FDA).
- **URL**: `https://accessgudid.nlm.nih.gov/` and `https://api.fda.gov/device/510k.json`
- **Terms of Use**: Data supplied by NLM/FDA is in the public domain.
- **Explicit Licensing Exclusions**:
  - **GMDN (Global Medical Device Nomenclature)** terms, codes, names, and definitions are excluded entirely due to third-party licensing restrictions.
  - **D-U-N-S (Data Universal Numbering System)** numbers are excluded.
- **Attribution Statement**:
  > "This product uses publicly available data courtesy of the U.S. National Library of Medicine (NLM) and the Food and Drug Administration (FDA). NLM and FDA are not responsible for the product's quality or performance, nor do they endorse this product."
- **Retrieval Date**: August 2026.
